A clock, alarm, stopwatch and timer for keypad phones like the Qin F21, Dumber Mini or Doov R77 Pro. Every screen, list and dialog works with the D-pad and the keypad: no floating buttons, nothing to drag or swipe, sized for a 480×640 screen. Touch is switched off inside the app, so nothing gets pressed by accident in your pocket.

<b>Keys</b>
• 1–5 – open Clock, Alarm, Stopwatch, Timer or Settings from the home list
• ← / → – switch an alarm off / on, start or pause a timer
• OK – open an alarm, start or pause the stopwatch
• Digits – type a time straight away (0730 = 07:30)
• Ringing alarm – ←, OK or Back snooze, → turns it off
• Finished timer – ← starts it again, → stops it
• Menu – the screen's other actions; a + row at the end of every list adds a new item

<b>Features</b>
• A home list that shows the time, the next alarm and a running stopwatch or timer
• Alarms with days to repeat, sound, vibration and a label
• Stopwatch with laps, timers with their own sound and label
• Your own and other time zones on the Clock screen
• The ringing alarm and a finished timer open on the whole screen, also on DumberOS without pop-up notifications
• System, light, dark and custom themes, purple or any other icon color
• Export and import of alarms and timers
• No ads, no tracking, no internet access

Based on Fossify Clock (GPL-3.0).
